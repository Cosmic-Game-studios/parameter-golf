# Breakthrough Plan

## North star
Reach or establish the strongest evidence-backed path toward a legal challenge LM at `0.8-0.9` shipped `val_bpb`.

## Frontier baseline to beat
- Best measured official in this repo: `1.51056383`
- Public open frontier is materially stronger than that and remains the practical bar for any real win
- Current local reproduced champion: `1.98453111`

## Novelty requirements
- Not another tiny donor polish unless it clearly reopens the family.
- At least one branch must aim for stronger scaling with more compute, not just a better local snapshot.
- Breakthrough claims must be backed by a branch that could plausibly survive official constraints.

## Branch portfolio
- Conservative:
  - maintain the reproduced `u4k share10 + bus20 + cross_skip` line as the local baseline and regression detector
- Orthogonal:
  - open a stronger effective-depth/shared-reuse branch that is more likely to scale on H100 than the current micro-control donor
- High-upside:
  - reopen tokenizer/token-flow or compression/training-stack ideas only when framed as a measurable, bounded screen
- Reproduction:
  - reproduce any new serious keep before promotion

## Ceiling hypotheses
- Tiny communication wins are real, but too small to explain a path to `0.9` by themselves.
- Stronger scaling likely needs a branch with more effective depth per byte and better training quality per minute.
- Official progress will also require stronger export/training-stack co-design than the active local winner uses today.

## Plateau protocol
- If two consecutive same-family micro-variants lose or only tie within noise, force an orthogonal branch next.
- If a local branch still cannot plausibly explain official scaling upside, keep it only as a baseline and move on.
